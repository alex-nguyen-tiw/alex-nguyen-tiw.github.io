3. First-Wash Cotton Shrinkage & Fabric Care Benchmarks

Even preshrunk heavyweight cotton experiences residual relaxation shrinkage during the first home wash-and-dry cycle. When a shopper falls within 1 inch (2.5 cm) of a size boundary, sizing up prevents post-wash tightness across the shoulders and midsection.
